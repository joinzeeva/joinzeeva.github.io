Community Rules

Aakhri badlav: 9 October 2026

Zeeva ek gumnaam jagah hai, par bina niyam ki jagah nahi. Ye niyam shuru se saaf likhe hain taaki baad me kisi ko shikayat na ho ki pata nahi tha. Inka paalan sabhi par barabar lagu hota hai.

01

Zeeva kis liye hai

Zeeva apne shehar, college, mohalle aur rozmarra ki samasyaon par khul kar baat karne ke liye hai. Bijli, sadak, paani, fees, exam, naukri, aspataal, thana, nagar palika. Yahan tumhara asli naam kabhi nahi dikhta, sirf ek ID dikhti hai.

02

Jo poori tarah azaad hai

  • Sarkar, prashasan, neta, vibhag aur unki yojnaon ki aalochana
  • Kisi adhikari ke kaam par sawal uthana
  • Apne ilaake ki samasya, shikayat aur anubhav batana
  • Asehmati, bahas aur alag raay

In cheezon par Zeeva par koi rok nahi hai. Isi ke liye ye bana hai.

03

Jo kabhi nahi chalega

  • Kisi asli, niji vyakti ka naam lekar aarop lagana. Kaam ki aalochana aur kisi par ilzaam lagana alag cheezein hain.
  • Dharm, jaati, ilaake ya samuday ko nishana banana. Kisi bhi roop me.
  • Hinsa, dhamki, ya ikattha hone ka bulawa — samay aur jagah ke saath. Zeeva baat karne ki jagah hai, kuch aayojit karne ki nahi.
  • Kisi ki niji jankari — mobile number, pata, gaadi number, pehchaan patra.
  • Police ya suraksha balon ki live location.

Aur ye, jo kanoon ke hisab se kahin bhi nahi chalte: balatkar ya bina sehmati ka koi kaam, 18 saal se kam umar wale se juda koi yaun vishay, aatankwad, drugs ka len-den, ya koi bhi aparadh.

04

Gumnaami ki seema

Tum doosre users ke liye gumnaam ho. Kanoon ke valid order ke saamne nahi.

Zeeva tumhara naam kisi user ko nahi dikhata. Lekin ek vaidh adalati ya sarkari aadesh aane par, kanoon ke mutabik humein jankari deni hoti hai. Isiliye upar wali cheezein yahan chalti hi nahi — gumnaami bachav ke liye hai, nuksan pahunchane ke liye nahi.

05

Report karne par kya hota hai

Har post par report ka option hai. Report hote hi wo post review me jaati hai. Agar ek hi post par kai report aayein to wo apne aap chhup jaati hai, jab tak uski jaanch na ho jaye.

Kanooni roop se galat content par post turant hatai jaati hai. Adalat ya sarkari aadesh aane par hum 36 ghante ke andar kaarravai karte hain.

06

Account par asar

Niyam todne par pehle chetavni, phir strike. 90 din me teen strike par account apne aap band ho jata hai. Gambhir maamlon me — jaise upar likhe gaye kanooni roop se galat vishay — pehli baar me hi account band aur zaroori hone par kanooni adhikariyon ko soochna.

07

Shikayat

Kisi content ya faisle se asehmat ho to Shikayat Adhikari ko likho. Hum 24 ghante me jawab dete hain aur 15 din me maamla nipta dete hain.
