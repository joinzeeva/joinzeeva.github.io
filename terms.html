Aakhri badlav: 9 October 2026

Zeeva ka istemal karke tum in sharton se sehmat hote ho. Chhoti bhasha me likhi hain, taaki padhi ja sakein.

01

Kaun istemal kar sakta hai

Sirf 18 saal ya usse upar ke log. Account banate waqt ye poochha jata hai. Galat umar batana in sharton ka ullanghan hai.

02

Tumhara account

Ek vyakti, ek account. Apna login kisi ko mat do. Account ki har gatividhi ki zimmedari us account wale ki hai. Agar lage ki kisi aur ne tumhara account khol liya hai, turant humein batao.

03

Tumhara content, tumhari zimmedari

Jo tum likhte ho wo tumhara hai. Uski kanooni zimmedari bhi tumhari hai. Zeeva use dikhane ke liye ek jagah deta hai, usse sehmat hone ka dawa nahi karta.

Humein itni ijazat chahiye ki hum tumhara content app ke andar dikha aur bhej sakein. Isse zyada kuch nahi, aur post hatate hi ye ijazat khatam.

04

Zeeva ki bhoomika

Zeeva ek intermediary hai, Information Technology Act, 2000 ke arth me. Hum content pehle se padhte ya chunte nahi hain. Jaise hi humein kisi galat content ki jaankari milti hai, hum niyam ke mutabik kaarravai karte hain.

05

Hum kya hata sakte hain

Koi bhi post ya account jo Community Rules todta hai. Adalati ya sarkari aadesh par 36 ghante ke andar. Aur wo content jo kanoon ke hisab se rakha hi nahi ja sakta, turant.

06

Ye abhi beta hai

Zeeva abhi testing me hai. Features badal sakte hain, seva kuch samay ke liye ruk sakti hai, aur beta ke dauraan banaya gaya data hamesha nahi bhi rah sakta. Hum pehle se batane ki koshish karenge.

07

Kanoon aur adhikar kshetra

In sharton par Bharat ka kanoon lagu hoga. Koi vivad hone par Sultanpur, Uttar Pradesh ki adalaton ka adhikar kshetra hoga.
