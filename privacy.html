Aakhri badlav: 9 October 2026

Ye batata hai ki hum kya lete hain, kyon lete hain, kitne din rakhte hain aur kisko dete hain. Chhoti si list hai, kyonki hum jaan-boojh kar bahut kam cheezein rakhte hain.

01

Abhi, is waitlist par

Sirf email address, jo tum khud dete ho. Isse hum sirf ek kaam karte hain: jab invite khulein tab tumhe batana. Na koi naam, na number, na location, na koi tracking ya advertising cookie is page par.

Page par jo ginti dikhti hai wo sirf ek sankhya hai — kiska email hai ye kabhi kisi ko nahi dikhta.

02

App me kya lenge

  • Email — account banane aur wapas login karne ke liye
  • Ek apne aap bani ID — jaise #7JR04LMN, jo doosre log dekhte hain
  • Jo tum post karte ho — likha hua text aur link
  • Takniki log — IP address aur samay, jo har website ke server par bante hain

Zeeva koi photo, video ya document apne server par nahi rakhta. Jo link tum post karte ho, wo apni hi jagah se load hota hai.

03

Kyon lete hain

Account chalane ke liye, galat istemal rokne ke liye, aur kanooni zarurat poori karne ke liye. Hum tumhara data kisi ko bechte nahi hain, aur kisi doosre vigyapan network ko nahi dete.

04

Kitne din rakhte hain

Account chalu rehne tak. Account delete karne par tumhari profile, posts aur chats hata di jaati hain.

Bharat ke niyam ke mutabik kuch buniyadi record — account banne ka samay, email ka ek surakshit nishan, aur post ka samay — 180 din tak rakhne padte hain, taaki baad me aayi kisi kanooni shikayat ki jaanch ho sake. Uske baad wo bhi mit jaate hain.

05

Kisko dete hain

Kisi ko nahi. Sirf do halat me:

  • Vaidh adalati ya sarkari aadesh par, utna hi jitna aadesh me maanga gaya ho
  • Un takniki sevaon ko jinke bina app chal hi nahi sakta (jaise hosting aur email bhejne wali seva)
06

Tumhare haq

Tum kabhi bhi apna data maang sakte ho, galat jankari theek karwa sakte ho, apna account delete kar sakte ho, aur waitlist se nikal sakte ho. Ek email bhej do, hum 15 din me kar denge.

07

Ek saaf baat suraksha par

Zeeva ke messages end-to-end encrypted nahi hain. Server par data encrypted roop me rakha jata hai, lekin hum ye dawa nahi karte ki hum use padh hi nahi sakte. Jo koi aisa dawa karta hai bina end-to-end encryption ke, wo sach nahi bol raha.

08

Shikayat Adhikari

Niji jankari se judi koi bhi baat ho to Shikayat Adhikari ko likho. 24 ghante me jawab, 15 din me nipatara.
